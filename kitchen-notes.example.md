# Kitchen notes

The menu job plans from this file each run. Copy it to `kitchen-notes.md` and
change it at any time. Only the `- ` lines under each heading are read. A
missing heading is an empty list.

## Standing rules

What holds every week.

- No fish on school nights; the kids will not eat it.
- Sunday is the one long cook of the week. Cook double, and carry half to Monday.
- Bulk meat goes in the freezer at put-away; pull it to the fridge the night before.

## Dinners we like

The model prefers these, and does not repeat last week's titles.

- Sheet-pan chicken thighs, potatoes and broccoli
- Crock chili with a toppings bar
- Smash burgers on the griddle
- Taco night with corn tortillas
- Pulled pork shoulder and coleslaw

## Staples

1 staple for each line, at most 60 characters. Each list must carry each one.

- Milk (2 gal)
- Eggs (2 dozen)
- Bananas
- Bread
- Coffee

## This week

Facts for this week only. A line with "until YYYY-MM-DD" drops out after that date.

- Grandma visits Saturday: 7 at the table. until 2026-10-18
