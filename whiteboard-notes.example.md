# Whiteboard notes

The whiteboard job reads this file each run and never writes it. Copy it to
`whiteboard-notes.md` and change it at any time.

## Standing notes

Facts that hold week to week. The notes step reads them. A line with
"until YYYY-MM-DD" drops out after that date.

- Sam drives the Tuesday and Thursday soccer runs; Alex has Wednesday.
- Leo's practice ride home is the Nguyen carpool on Thursdays. until 2026-11-15

## Overrides

1 line for each day that the custody calendar has wrong:
`- YYYY-MM-DD | kid | home or away | reason`

- 2026-10-16 | Maya | home | Jordan out of town
